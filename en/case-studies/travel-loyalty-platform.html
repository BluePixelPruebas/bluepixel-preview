Case study · Latin American airline

One sign-in, miles in plain sight and benefits on visual cards.

Access on a single screen, with information on one side and context imagery on the other; an account built around miles; benefits on visual cards.

1 single screen

to sign in or sign up, which used to be separate journeys

Industry
Travel and loyalty
Service
03 · Digital Platforms
Specialties
UX · UI · Web development · Responsive product · Loyalty platforms
Challenge and solution

Where we started and what we did

Context

Redesign the loyalty program section to improve usability, modernize the interface and streamline sign-in and registration, so managing miles, benefits and the account would be clearer.

The challenge

Friction across the key journeys of the loyalty platform, from sign-in to checking benefits.

What we did

  1. Requirements analysis
  2. Research and insight extraction
  3. Information architecture restructuring
  4. Wireframing
  5. UI discovery and visual design
Results

What changed

Increase in mobile session time
+22%

  • Clearer navigation and content hierarchy
  • Faster sign-in and account management
