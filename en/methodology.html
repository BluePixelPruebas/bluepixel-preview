Frequently asked questions

About IMPATH™ and FutureProof™

What is IMPATH™?

It is BluePixel’s proprietary methodology for finding the friction that costs an operation the most: it builds digital twins of your users that walk through your flows and show where time and money are lost. It is delivered as a friction diagnostic within Digital Consulting and serves as the basis for design in the other ways of working.

How do IMPATH™ and FutureProof™ relate?

IMPATH™ is the method for finding the friction that costs the most; FutureProof™ is the guarantee of how whatever comes out of that diagnostic is built. The five principles are on the FutureProof page.

What do I receive from an operational friction diagnostic?

A friction diagnostic with digital twins (IMPATH™), the recommended technical architecture, a backlog prioritized by financial impact, and a business case with projected ROI and cost of inaction.
