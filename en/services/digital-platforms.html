About hiring us

Does it really fit in 2 to 4 months?

Yes, if the scope is narrowed. The flow that creates value is built first and the rest waits; the exact timeline is set when we agree on the scope.

Do you build both web and mobile?

Yes: web platforms and applications, and native or cross-platform mobile apps. To decide which one suits you, use the mobile apps and web development guides.

What happens after launch?

We hand over the code and documentation so your team can continue, or we carry on with Digital Evolution (Service 04). You decide.

Can the platform include AI agents?

Yes. The architecture is prepared to integrate them from the start. If you want agents on the systems you already have, that is Service 02.
