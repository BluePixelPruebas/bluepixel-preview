Services

Software, AI and digital product services for companies

Four ways to work with BluePixel, depending on what you need to solve today. None depends on another, and in all of them we reduce risk, build with evidence and evolve without starting from scratch.

  1. Service 01 2 to 4 weeks

    Digital Consulting

    Do you know what to build and what it will return? Technology and product strategy to define what to build, automate or evolve before investing.
    • IMPATH™ operational friction diagnosis
    • Backlog prioritized by financial impact
    • Business case with projected ROI and cost of inaction
    See the service →
  2. Service 02 2 to 4 weeks

    Agents & Automation

    For operations with repetitive tasks and disconnected systems Process and operations automation with AI agents integrated into the systems your company already uses.
    • AI agents in production in 2 to 4 weeks
    • Integration with SAP, Salesforce, ERP and CRM via MCP
    • Private RAG over your company’s real data
    See the service →
  3. Service 03 2 to 4 months

    Digital Platforms

    To build platforms and MVPs from scratch with validated UX Custom software development, web platforms, mobile apps and enterprise MVPs from scratch, with validated UX.
    • Prototyping and validation with real users
    • Cloud-native Full Stack development with a decoupled architecture
    • Automated testing and deployment with a 99.9% SLA
    See the service →
  4. Service 04 6 or 12 months

    Digital Evolution

    To evolve existing platforms with continuous optimization and CRO Modernization, optimization and continuous development of existing software and platforms, with a dedicated squad and a living roadmap.
    • Tech Lead, AI Engineer, Senior Full Stack and UX/CRO Specialist
    • UX Health Score and technical debt reduction
    • Conversion optimization and support with an enterprise SLA
    See the service →
