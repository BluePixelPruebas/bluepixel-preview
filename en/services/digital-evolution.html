About hiring us

What is the minimum commitment?

A living roadmap of 6 or 12 months, reviewed every quarter.

Can we change the squad’s profiles?

Yes. If one quarter needs more AI and another more UX, the mix of specialists is adjusted.

How does it compare with hiring our own team?

You avoid recruiting, retaining and coordinating four different senior profiles, and the squad arrives with its method. Cost is compared with your numbers when we agree on the scope.

Does it work if another vendor built the platform?

Yes. We start by learning the code and measuring the baseline before proposing changes.
