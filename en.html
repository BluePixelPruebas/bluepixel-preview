Which service should we start with?

It depends on where your company is. If you do not yet know what to build or with what return, start with Digital Consulting. If your systems work but your team does repetitive work, with Agents & Automation. If you have a product to build, with Digital Platforms. And if your platform is already in production and needs to keep improving, with Digital Evolution.

Does BluePixel replace the systems I already have?

No. We work on what you already have running: we connect agents and platforms to your ERP, your CRM and your APIs through open protocols such as MCP, and everything lives in your cloud.

What kind of companies work with BluePixel?

Companies in Mexico and Latin America that need software, automation or AI with a measurable business result, in sectors such as retail, travel, entertainment and financial services. You can see examples in the case studies.

What happens in the 20-minute call?

You talk through your case with someone from the technical team, with no sales commitment. At the end we tell you which service fits, with its scope and timeline. It is an orientation conversation, not the Digital Consulting diagnosis.
